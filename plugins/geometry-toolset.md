<h2>Description / 描述</h2>
<p>Gives AI assistants the ability to author procedural geometry — spawn DynamicMesh actors, append primitive shapes (boxes, cylinders, cones, spheres, capsules, tori), and compose them with boolean operations (union, subtract, intersect). Designed as a substrate-level toolset that other toolsets (BlockoutToolset, future Vehicle/Vegetation toolsets) can compose with. Editor-only.</p>
