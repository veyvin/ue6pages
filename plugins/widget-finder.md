<h2>Description / 描述</h2>
<p>Find live Slate widgets by query. Mirrors the running widget tree into a queryable store and adds a browser panel that searches it and highlights matches on screen. Runs in the editor, under -game, and in a packaged Development build; absent from Shipping/Test, where Slate debugging is compiled out.</p>
