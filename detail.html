Description 描述

Accumulation Depth of Field is a high-quality, thin-lens aperture-sampled depth of field effect designed for production rendering in Unreal Engine. It uses a physically-based lens model that traces rays through a simulated camera aperture, accumulating samples to produce natural, film-like bokeh.

该插件实现了基于薄透镜模型的景深效果，通过对相机光圈进行采样和累积，生成接近真实电影镜头的焦外成像（bokeh）。支持多种光圈形状、焦平面过渡以及基于距离的后景/前景模糊控制。

Key Features / 核心特性

  • Physically-based thin-lens sampling without costly ray tracing passes
  • Configurable aperture shape (circular, hexagonal, octal) and aperture blade count
  • Foreground and background blur are independently controllable with falloff curves
  • Integrates with Movie Render Queue for path-traced offline renders at any quality level
  • Exposure compensation and chromatic aberration simulation for cinematic realism

Performance / 性能

On a modern GPU, real-time usage typically costs 0.8–1.4 ms at 1080p depending on blur radius and sample count. Movie Render Queue accumulation scales sub-linearly with sample count — 128 samples for near-noiseless output.

Use Cases 适用场景

Most commonly used in Virtual Production workflows to replicate real-camera characteristics in LED volume shoots, and in cinematic pre-render pipelines for trailers and film sequences. Also suited for architectural visualization and high-end automotive renders.
